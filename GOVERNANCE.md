# YottaDots Governance

## Current phase

YottaDots is currently founder-led.

This is intentional during the early validation phase.

## Responsibilities

The founder currently maintains responsibility for:

- canonical core;
- releases;
- quality standards;
- trademark;
- official materials;
- governance evolution.

## Community

The community can propose, discuss, review and develop content.

## Long-term direction

Governance should progressively move toward:

Founder

→ Maintainers

→ Contributor Council

→ Community-owned governance

The governance model will evolve as the community grows.
