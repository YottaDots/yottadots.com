# Governance

YottaDots starts as a founder-led open community.

The long-term ambition is community ownership.

## v0.1 governance

The founder currently maintains:

- strategic direction;
- canonical core;
- quality standards;
- releases;
- trademark;
- official training standards.

Community members can:

- propose changes;
- create extensions;
- review content;
- contribute patterns;
- challenge existing ideas.

## Future governance

As the community grows, governance will gradually move toward:

1. maintainers;
2. contributor councils;
3. domain stewards;
4. independent community governance.

The goal is not permanent founder control.

The goal is a sustainable open ecosystem.
