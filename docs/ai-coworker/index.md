# AI as a Co-worker

![Visual summary. Left: adding AI to an existing five-step process keeps the same steps and hand-offs, with AI attached to each step. Right: redesigning the work, where a human and AI together handle the work and steps two to four are no longer needed. Below: the questions to answer for every piece of work.](/img/diagrams/ai-coworker-summary.svg)

AI changes more than productivity.

It changes the design of work itself.

YottaDots explores how humans, teams and AI agents can work together while maintaining clear accountability.

Questions include:

- What decisions can AI support?
- What work can AI perform autonomously?
- Where must human judgment remain?
- Who owns the outcome?
- What guardrails are required?
- How does organizational design change when coordination becomes cheaper?

AI should not simply be added to existing processes.

Sometimes the process itself should disappear.
