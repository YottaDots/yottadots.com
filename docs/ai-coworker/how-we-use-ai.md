# How YottaDots uses AI

YottaDots says AI should be a co-worker. This website is built that way, and
we want you to be able to see how.

## What AI does

AI (currently mainly Claude, by Anthropic) helps to:

- draft and rewrite pages;
- research sources and compare YottaDots with other models;
- check content for contradictions, gaps and unsupported claims;
- build and maintain the website code;
- prepare every change as a proposal (a pull request) for human review.

## What humans do

- **Decide.** Canonical Core decisions, positioning and releases are made by
  the founder, not by AI.
- **Review.** Every change, from AI or from a contributor, is reviewed by a
  human maintainer before it is published. Nothing goes live without that
  approval.
- **Take responsibility.** The founder holds editorial responsibility for
  everything published on this website.

## How you can see it

- Changes made with AI are marked in the repository history: each such commit
  carries a `Co-Authored-By` line naming the AI.
- Claims without supporting evidence are labelled **[Needs Evidence]**. AI can
  be wrong, and so can people.
- Found an error? [Suggest an improvement](https://github.com/YottaDots/yottadots.com/issues/new?template=improve-page.yml).

## What is not made by AI

The YottaDots logo was created by a human designer and further developed by the
founder, without AI.

## Using AI when you contribute

You may use AI when you contribute. You remain responsible for what you
submit: check it, make sure you have the right to share it, and mention that
you used AI. See the
[contribution terms](https://github.com/YottaDots/yottadots.com/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#using-ai).
