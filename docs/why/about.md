## Why I started YottaDots

Working in and with many organizations, I kept running into the same
problems:

- **Decisions are not made, or made too late.**
- **The top does not see the real work:** what it takes, how long it takes,
  what technology makes possible.
- **People are talked about, not talked with.**
- **Information gets softer on its way up.** It is burning in the basement,
  the top hears that it is getting warm downstairs, and orders an air
  conditioner.
- **More and more measuring,** to feel in control.
- **Hiring more managers when there is a problem,** when a conversation, some
  trust and a good decision would solve it.
- **Valuable work has to fight for every penny,** while projects nobody needs
  get the dime.

## Looking for a better way

I studied other methods and frameworks. SAFe has good ideas, but why should
it cover the whole company? How would marketing or sales work in Scrum? Have
you ever seen a shipyard work in Scrum? I would be curious what that looks
like.

What I learned is that the people doing the work can best decide how to do
it: Scrum, Kanban, projects, or a good old planning board. What counts is the
result, not the route. Somewhere along the way many organizations lost that.
The method, the framework or the role became more important than the
outcome.

## What YottaDots does

YottaDots is my answer: a federative way of organizing.

- **The centre decides the what:** clear choices and ranked priorities.
- **Value streams decide the how,** because they know the work.
- **Guardrails apply to everyone:** laws, security, quality of work, and
  measurement, so everything stays visible.
- **The outcome counts,** not the activity.

Read more in [Why YottaDots?](./index.md) and
[The YottaDots Method](../method/index.md), or see everything on one page in
[The Big Picture](../method/big-picture.md).

## AI as a co-worker

I see AI taking over more and more of the work that exists to coordinate
other work: collecting, reporting, passing information on. Much of what
middle management does today. As a co-worker, AI can often do that faster,
better and at lower cost. People remain needed, and accountable, for the
human side of work: judgment, trust, relationships and care.

## Open, and still young

YottaDots is open: free to use under [CC BY 4.0](../community/license.md),
and built together [on GitHub](https://github.com/YottaDots/yottadots.com). It
is also young (v0.1), and its patterns have not yet been tested in practice.
If you would like to try it in your organization, I would like to hear from
you.
