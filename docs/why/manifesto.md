# YottaDots Manifesto

**Federated. AI-native. Outcome-driven.**

Members align together, act independently and learn from evidence, with AI as
a co-worker and humans accountable for outcomes.

## We believe in

1. **AI as a co-worker.** AI is a visible, accountable partner in analysis,
   code and decisions.
2. **Independent members, shared core.** Members agree together on a shared
   direction. They delegate to a shared core only what must be shared, and
   everything else stays with them.
3. **Outcomes over output.** Value delivered counts, not activity reported.
4. **Transparency.** Data, decisions and learning are open.
5. **Empiricism.** We observe, measure and adapt. Evidence beats opinion.
6. **Freedom of method.** Members and their teams choose the way of working
   that fits their mission: Scrum, Kanban, PRINCE2, DevOps, release trains or
   something else.
7. **Lightweight governance.** Guardrails enable flow; they do not add
   bureaucracy.
8. **Human accountability.** AI strengthens people. People stay responsible.
9. **Safety and quality by design.** Security, privacy and quality are built
   in from the start.
10. **Community and openness.** We share patterns so everyone gets better.

## We value

| | over | |
|---|---|---|
| **People and AI working together** | over | rigid roles and hierarchy |
| **Adaptability and autonomy** | over | standard processes for their own sake |
| **Evidence and outcomes** | over | effort and intentions |
| **Clarity and trust** | over | control and opacity |
| **Reuse and openness** | over | reinvention and silos |

While there is value in the items on the right, we value the items on the left
more.

## We commit to

1. **Agree on intent together.** Members set a shared direction and delegate
   to the shared core only what must be shared. Members and their teams own
   the rest.
2. **Make work visible**, so flow, risk and value can be seen by everyone.
3. **Let members and teams choose their method** and own the full lifecycle.
4. **Use AI throughout**, from portfolio forecasts to code and tests.
5. **Inspect and adapt** at a regular rhythm and when signals appear, with
   small bets and fast feedback.
6. **Build in safety** with security, privacy and quality gates.
7. **Share playbooks.** Keep what works and retire what does not.
8. **Measure what matters:** customer outcomes, time to learning and the total
   cost of change.
9. **Respect human judgment.** Override AI when the context requires it, and
   record why.
10. **Grow people:** skills, mobility and mastery across teams.
