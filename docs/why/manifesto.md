# YottaDots Manifesto

We believe organizations should be designed around capability, trust and purpose.

We believe decisions should be made as close as possible to the knowledge required to make them.

We believe autonomy needs alignment.

We believe governance should enable rather than control.

We believe AI will become a co-worker, not merely another tool.

We believe organizational knowledge should be open, reusable and continuously improved.

And we believe better organizations are built together.

## Open knowledge. Better together.
