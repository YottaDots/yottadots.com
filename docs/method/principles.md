# Principles

YottaDots v0.1 starts with four principles.

## Federate

Place authority close to knowledge and value creation.

## Align

Create shared purpose, principles and guardrails.

## Augment

Use AI to strengthen human capability and reduce coordination overhead.

## Evolve

Treat the organization as a system that continuously learns and adapts.

---

These principles are intentionally simple.

The community will help refine them.
