# Principles

YottaDots v0.1 starts with four principles. Together they describe how a
YottaDots organization works: who decides what, how direction is shared,
what AI is for, and how the design itself keeps improving.

![Overview of the four principles. A centre at the top and four value streams below it. Federate is the split between them: the centre decides the what, the value streams decide the how. Align is the two-way arrows between them: goals go down, input and objections come up. Augment is a band of AI co-workers across the whole organization, reducing coordination work, with a human owner for every result. Evolve is a loop around everything: the design is tried, reviewed and changed.](/img/diagrams/principles-overview.svg)

## Federate

The centre decides the what; value streams decide the how. Everything not
named as central stays with the value streams.
[Read more →](./principles/federate.md)

## Align

The centre proposes shared goals, principles and guardrails. Value streams
help shape them: they can object, propose and experiment.
[Read more →](./principles/align.md)

## Augment

Use AI to strengthen human capability and reduce coordination overhead.
[Read more →](./principles/augment.md)

## Evolve

Treat the organization as a system that continuously learns and adapts.
[Read more →](./principles/evolve.md)

## How the principles work together

- **Federate and Align** are two sides of one balance. Federate gives value
  streams freedom; Align keeps that freedom pointed in a shared direction.
  Either one alone fails: freedom without alignment fragments, alignment
  without freedom is a hierarchy.
- **Augment** makes the balance cheaper to keep. Much of the cost of
  alignment is coordination: meetings, reports, hand-offs. AI can take on
  part of that work.
- **Evolve** keeps the whole design honest. The list of central decisions,
  the guardrails and the patterns are all reviewed and changed when they stop
  working.
