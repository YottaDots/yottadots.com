# YottaDots Licensing

YottaDots is intended to become an open knowledge project.

The final licensing architecture is currently being prepared.

The intended model is:

- open core knowledge and documentation;
- open community contributions;
- separately governed official materials;
- separately governed YottaDots trademarks and badges;
- permissively licensed software where applicable.

Until the final licenses and contribution terms have been published,
please contact the YottaDots maintainers before redistributing modified
official YottaDots materials.

A formal licensing release will follow before YottaDots v1.0.
